Our story

Counsel that has sat on both sides of the table.

VidhiCorp Legal Chambers is led by Jankee Ballabh Pandey, who has spent two decades moving between courtroom practice and in-house counsel roles at multinational businesses, most recently as Head of Legal & Integrity Officer at Atotech, with earlier roles at Unilever, Whirlpool and Trident.

That dual view shapes every engagement: advice weighed against what the business can actually absorb, not just what the law permits.

Mission: deliver precise, commercially grounded counsel with the discretion our clients are entitled to expect.

How a matter moves

From first meeting to final report.

The same process, every time. Nothing skipped because a matter looks routine.

01

Understand

A first meeting and a first read of the documents, so advice starts from the real matter, not a summary of it.

02

Advise

The available recourse, laid out in plain terms, with a cost estimate before anything is committed to.

03

Investigate

Facts and evidence gathered and tested against what the client actually needs to prove or protect.

04

Deliver

Regular updates, a shared draft, feedback folded in, closed out with a final report, not a loose end.

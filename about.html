A chambers built on foresight, not firefighting.

Two decades of combined partner experience across courtroom practice, transactional advisory and in-house counsel roles, brought together under one name.

Our story

Counsel that has sat on both sides of the table.

A full-service legal practice serving individuals, businesses and corporates across India. Our founding partners have argued before courts and tribunals, and sat inside the businesses they advised as in-house counsel.

That dual view shapes every engagement: advice weighed against what the business can actually absorb, not just what the law permits.

Mission: deliver precise, commercially grounded counsel with the discretion our clients are entitled to expect.

Vision

To be the counsel a business calls before a decision is made. Not the counsel it calls after one goes wrong.

What guides us

Four commitments, held to deliberately.

We chose four, not eleven. Each one is something a client can hold us to.

01

Foresight

Anticipating the exposure before it becomes a matter is the discipline the chambers is built around, not a marketing line.

02

Commercial understanding

Our partners have run compliance calendars and reviewed contracts from inside a business, not only from outside it.

03

Precision

We would rather take longer over a document than have it fail the one time it is tested.

04

Discretion

Confidentiality is not a clause in our engagement letter. It is the basis on which clients speak freely with us.

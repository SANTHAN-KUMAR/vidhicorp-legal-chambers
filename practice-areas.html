Practice areas in detail

What each area actually covers.

A closer look at the matters and sub-services within each of the thirteen practice areas.

Corporate & Commercial Advisory

Corporate governance, legal compliance and risk management; contract management; business valuation, accounting and auditing support; business structuring and restructuring; ESOP solutions; international trade advisory.

Transactions & Strategic Advisory

Domestic and cross-border transaction structuring; contract drafting and review; financing and investment support; real estate and employment-linked transaction advisory; strategic business advisory across the deal lifecycle.

Contracts & Agreements

Employment and sales contracts, lease agreements, partnership deeds, AOA/MOA and other corporate documents, shareholder agreements, NDAs, settlement and arbitration agreements, powers of attorney.

Related reading: Reading the indemnity clause the way your counterparty will →

Joint Ventures

Structuring and negotiation of joint ventures and strategic alliances, JV agreements, governance arrangements between partners, and dispute-avoidance mechanisms built into the documentation from the outset.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Pre-transaction due diligence and valuation; negotiation of purchase, merger and shareholder agreements, NDAs and letters of intent; regulatory compliance and financing; post-transaction integration and governance.

Cross-Border Transactions

International contract drafting and review, trade compliance, cross-border M&A and foreign investment structuring, regulatory approvals, tax planning, and currency and exchange-control considerations.

Funding & Investments

Loan agreements and debt fundraising, equity and securities offerings, pre- and post-IPO advisory, employee stock option plans, and fintech and digital-securities advisory.

Related reading: ESOP pools: sizing them before you need them →

Employment, HR & POSH

Employment contracts, offer and appointment letters, handbooks and policies, disciplinary proceedings and terminations, union and workplace-safety matters, and full POSH services including external ICC membership, awareness training and inquiry reports.

Related reading: POSH compliance beyond the policy document →

Compliance & Regulatory Support

Corporate secretarial compliance, board resolutions and governance, regulatory filings and licensing, HR and data-privacy compliance, contract lifecycle management, and SEBI and stock-exchange compliance advisory.

Related reading: Building a compliance calendar that survives a growing entity →

Dispute Resolution & Arbitration

Civil and criminal litigation, arbitration and mediation, recovery proceedings including Section 138 matters, settlement negotiations, and police-complaint and FIR assistance, always weighed against what protects the business.

Related reading: When arbitration is faster than litigation, and when it isn't →

Due Diligence

Document, litigation and regulatory-compliance review; intellectual-property and employment/labour review; environmental due diligence; risk assessment reporting for transactions, property and counterparties.

Intellectual Property Rights

Trademark, patent and copyright searches, filing and prosecution; enforcement and infringement litigation; trade-secret protection; IP portfolio management, audits, licensing strategy and anti-counterfeiting action.

Related reading: Trademark renewal timelines: what businesses routinely get wrong →

Legal Opinions

Considered, written legal opinions to support high-stakes business decisions, board resolutions and transaction sign-off, drawing on the other twelve practice areas as the matter requires.

Practice areas

Thirteen areas of focused practice, organised the way problems actually arrive at our desk: rarely as a single, isolated question. Each is led by counsel with direct transactional or courtroom experience.

Corporate & Commercial Advisory

Corporate governance, contract management, business structuring and legal due diligence for growing enterprises.

Transactions & Strategic Advisory

Business advisory, transaction structuring and legal opinions across the deal lifecycle.

Contracts & Agreements

Drafting, negotiation and review of commercial contracts, shareholder agreements and transaction documents.

Joint Ventures

Structuring and documentation for joint ventures and strategic alliances.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Due diligence, valuation, deal structuring and post-transaction integration support.

Cross-Border Transactions

International contracts, trade compliance, and cross-border M&A and financing.

Funding & Investments

Loan agreements, equity and debt fundraising, and investment transaction support.

Employment, HR & POSH

Employment contracts, workplace policies, disciplinary proceedings and POSH / ICC investigations.

Compliance & Regulatory Support

Corporate, HR, data-privacy and secretarial compliance, board resolutions and regulatory filings.

Dispute Resolution & Arbitration

Litigation, arbitration, mediation and settlement negotiation to protect business interests.

Due Diligence

Transaction, property and counterparty diligence with clear, decision-ready reporting.

Intellectual Property Rights

Trademark, copyright and patent registration, portfolio management and infringement action.

Legal Opinions

Considered legal opinions to support complex or high-stakes business decisions — the thirteenth area, and often where the other twelve begin.

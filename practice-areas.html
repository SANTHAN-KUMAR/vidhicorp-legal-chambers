Practice areas

Twelve areas of focused practice, organised the way problems actually arrive at our desk: rarely as a single, isolated question. Each is led by counsel with direct transactional or courtroom experience.

Corporate & Commercial Advisory

Entity structuring, shareholder and founder arrangements, joint ventures, governance frameworks and day-to-day commercial contracting.

Mergers, Acquisitions & Private Equity

Deal structuring, transaction documentation, negotiation and closing support across the acquisition and investment lifecycle.

Banking & Finance

Loan and security documentation, project finance, regulatory filings and lender-borrower negotiations.

Dispute Resolution & Arbitration

Pre-litigation strategy, domestic and institutional arbitration, and commercial litigation managed to protect the underlying relationship.

Regulatory & Compliance

Compliance calendars mapped to the statutes that actually apply to your entity type, sector and scale, plus internal policy design.

Intellectual Property Rights

Trademark, copyright and trade-dress registration, portfolio management and enforcement action.

Employment & Labour Law

Policy design, workforce restructuring, POSH compliance and dispute avoidance for employers across sectors.

Real Estate & Infrastructure

Title diligence, project documentation, RERA compliance and infrastructure development agreements.

Media, Entertainment & Technology

Content licensing, data privacy and platform agreements for media, entertainment and technology businesses.

Startup & Venture Advisory

Incorporation, cap-table structuring, ESOP design and funding-round documentation for founder-led businesses.

White-Collar Investigations

Internal inquiries, regulatory response and advisory on matters of corporate fraud and code-of-conduct breaches.

Due Diligence & Risk Advisory

Transaction, property and counterparty diligence with plain-language, red-flag reporting for decision-makers.

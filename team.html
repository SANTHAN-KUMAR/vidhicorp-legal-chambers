How the chambers is organised

Every matter is led by a partner.

The chambers is structured in tiers of experience so that a matter always has senior oversight, while day-to-day execution is resourced efficiently.

Partners

Own client relationships and matter strategy start to finish. Every engagement letter names a partner directly accountable to you.

Senior Associates

Lead drafting, diligence and procedural work under partner supervision, and act as your day-to-day point of contact.

Associates

Research, documentation and filings, keeping matters moving without unnecessary billing overhead.

Associate Network

Independent counsel and advocates across other Indian cities, engaged where a matter needs local presence.

How the chambers is organised

Every matter is led by a partner.

The chambers is structured in tiers of experience so that a matter always has senior oversight, while day-to-day execution is resourced efficiently.

Partners

Own client relationships and matter strategy from the first conversation to resolution. Every engagement letter names a partner directly accountable to the client.

Senior Associates

Lead drafting, diligence and procedural work under partner supervision, and are typically the day-to-day point of contact once a matter is underway.

Associates

Support research, documentation and filings, working closely with senior associates to keep matters moving without unnecessary billing overhead.

Associate Network

A network of independent counsel and advocates across other Indian cities, engaged as specific matters require local presence or specialist expertise.

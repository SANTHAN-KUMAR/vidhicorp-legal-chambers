Before you write in

A few common questions

Does the chambers handle disputes as well as advisory work?

Yes. While we're built around preventive, advisory work, our partners also run pre-litigation strategy, arbitration and commercial litigation when a matter can't be resolved without it.

Which cities do you serve?

We're based in Gurugram and advise clients across India through a network of associate counsel in other cities, engaged as specific matters require.

How do I engage the firm for a new matter?

Send an enquiry through the form above, by email or by phone. A partner reviews it personally and responds within one business day.

Is the initial conversation chargeable?

No. The initial conversation to understand your matter and confirm it's within our practice areas is not chargeable.
